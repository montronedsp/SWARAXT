# Neon Cobalt assets

These retained-vector runtime assets are generated from the canonical Neon Cobalt design and the shared Swara XT lockup geometry by `scripts/normalize-svg-assets.ps1`.

- The backdrop preserves the Neon Cobalt atmosphere, texture, and blue/violet field treatment.
- The lockup uses the exact Midnight Gold design geometry with Neon Cobalt artwork colours.
- Frames, braids, and ornaments share their geometry with Midnight Gold and use the Neon Cobalt semantic treatment.
